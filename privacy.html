Privacy Policy

Runway To Cockpit Uploader · last updated 29 October 2026

"Runway To Cockpit Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Runway To Cockpit to publish the channel's own lessons. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What the tool accesses

  • The owner's own YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API, to upload and schedule the channel's lessons and Shorts, and to add their captions, thumbnails and playlist entries.
  • The owner's own channel statistics, through the YouTube Analytics API (for example views and watch time), to decide which topics to make next.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process data about viewers or any other Google users.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not use the data for advertising.

How access is stored

The sign-in permission (an OAuth refresh token) is kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's GitHub repository and is used only by that repository's automated jobs. The owner can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
