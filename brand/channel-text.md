## YouTube: "About" description (paste as is, under 1,000 characters)
Clear, visual ground school for student pilots, one lesson every day at 10:15 AM IST.

We start with DGCA Air Regulations: the Chicago Convention, ICAO, Rules of the Air, airspace, separation, aerodromes, licensing and India's Bharatiya Vayuyan Adhiniyam, explained with original 3D animation, illustrated airports and exam traps from previous papers. Every lesson ends with a quick quiz and a recap you can revise from.

Preparing for EASA or UK CAA exams? The ICAO foundations are identical, and we flag where the rules differ.

New lesson every day · Subscribe and turn on notifications.

Facts are checked against ICAO, the Gazette of India and DGCA publications. Always confirm current figures in the latest official documents.
